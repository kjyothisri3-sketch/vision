    Responsible AI

    “AI That Knows When It Isn't Sure.”

    AI systems can sometimes make incorrect predictions. VisionMate communicates confidence levels and warns the user when a prediction may be uncertain.

    High Confidence

    90–100%

    Announced as a direct statement, e.g. “Person detected ahead.”

    Medium Confidence

    60–89%

    Flagged as “medium confidence” so the user knows to stay alert.

    Low Confidence

    Below 60%

    Phrased as a possibility with a warning to verify manually.

    “VisionMate assists human decision-making. It does not replace human judgment.”

    User

    What each stage does

    1. Camera Frames captured via getUserMedia (browser) or the glasses' sensor.
    2. Image Processing Resize, normalise and prepare frames for inference.
    3. AI / Computer Vision A vision model scores what it sees (COCO-SSD in Live mode).
    4. Object + Text Detection Bounding boxes and labels; OCR is on the roadmap.
    5. Confidence Analysis Results are banded High / Medium / Low.
    6. Voice / Text Output A short sentence via SpeechSynthesis and on-screen text.
    7. User Decides, with the AI as an assistant rather than an authority.
